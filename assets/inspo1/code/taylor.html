Three Steps

Process

Process

From idea to ink — here’s how Taylor will bring your tattoo vision to life.

BOOK EXPERIENCE
VIEW GALLERY

[ 01 ]

share your vision

Before we begin, it’s important to align on your idea and goals. Whether it’s your first tattoo or part of an existing collection, this step is all about connection and understanding.

- Talk through your concept, inspiration, and story

- Share any reference photos or themes

- Align on the direction and meaning

[ 02 ]

Book your session

Once the design is clear and aligned, we'll get your appointment locked in. A deposit will secure your spot (one deposit required per session scheduled) and we'll prepare for the appointment ahead. Deposits are non-refundable unless Taylor cancels on his end. *Note: The estimated price given during consultation is an estimate and may be adjusted based on the final size and placement of the design.

- Approve the final design, size, and placement

- Receive estimated price for concept

- Pay your deposit to confirm your date

[ 03 ]

tattoo session

Your tattoo day is about comfort, quality, and focus. Taylor works with precision and intention to deliver work that lasts — and makes sure you’re set up for a smooth healing process.

- Tattoo session at the studio

- Clear aftercare walkthrough

- Leave with a bold, meaningful new piece
